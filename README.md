# Electric Vehicle (EV) Market Performance & Insights

An executive-level Tableau dashboard built to evaluate global EV market trends, battery technology adoption, manufacturer market share, and customer satisfaction across three key stakeholder perspectives (Emily Johnson, David Chen, Dr. Sarah Williams).

## 👥 Stakeholder Scenarios Addressed
1. **Emily Johnson (Market Strategy Director):** Evaluates top-selling vehicle categories, leading manufacturers, and customer satisfaction segments.
2. **David Chen (Business Intelligence Manager):** Analyzes manufacturer market share, annual sales trends, and global manufacturing country distribution.
3. **Dr. Sarah Williams (Sustainable Mobility Research Analyst):** Examines battery technology adoption trends, energy efficiency, and driving range improvements.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
* **Tableau:** Interactive dashboard design and multi-persona storytelling.
* **GitHub:** Version control and project documentation.
